Project Overview

Objectives

  • The objective of this project is to enhance the real-world usability of the portable potentiostat board by improving its power efficiency.
  • Currently, the board's firmware is not optimized for power consumption causing the board to drain its battery quicker than desired.
  • The problem I am trying to address is the relatively low battery life of the device.

Technical Approach

  • The technical approach is inspecting the data sheets of the LMP91000 and EFR32 and reviewing the built-in power modes.
  • I will also explore time delays in transmitting data over Bluetooth.

Research Directions

  • The first goal is to develop a storage/hibernation mode for the device that can be activated with the push button.
  • I will work with the power modes available on the microconroller to improve power efficiency while in use.

Expected Accomplishments

  • The goal of this research is to improve the time this device can be used without needing to be recharged.
  • This device needs a decent battery life to be applicable in the field.
