Project Overview

Objectives

  • I am doing this work to improve the power efficiency of the portable potentiostat board to enhance its real-world usability.
  • Otherwise, this board will lose power during use.
  • The problem I am trying to address is the sensor dying while in operation.

Technical Approach

  • I am doing this work by inspecting the data sheets of the LMP91000 and EFR32 and utilizing the built-in power modes. I will also explore time delays in transmitting data over Bluetooth to improve power efficiency.

Research Directions

  • There is information on the internet about power optimizing embedded systems. However, the methods are fairly complicated.
  • I will implement the power optimization techniques in this potentiostat project.

Expected Accomplishments

  • The goal of this research is to improve the maximum time this device can be used without needing to be recharged.
  • This device needs a decent battery life to be applicable in the field.
