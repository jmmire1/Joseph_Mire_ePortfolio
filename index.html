About Me

A little more about who I am and what drives me.

Background

Currently a Junior at the University of Alabama, I balance my engineering curriculum with active leadership and community involvement. I am the Vice-President and co-founder of Ripping Tide, UA's drone racing team, where I teach students how to build, fly, and program FPV drones. Additionally, I hold my FAA Part 107 Commercial Drone License and stay very active with Bama Catholic, my school's Catholic community.

Interests

My technical interests heavily revolve around coding, FPV drone racing, 3D printing, and homelabbing. I have experience building several racing drones alongside a custom 5-inch freestyle quad. Additionally, I built a Voron 0.2 3D printer from scratch and maintain a Proxmox server to host various services such as Immich and Home Assistant.

Currently Learning

I am constantly looking to expand my technical and life horizons, and am currently focusing on advancing my knowledge of drones, web development, and network infrastructure. Additionally, I am exploring the best ways to integrate AI in my learning and projects.
